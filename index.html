ROLLIE'S Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026 · Operated by MAYACREW Inc.

ROLLIE'S (the "app") is a film-camera style photo app. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and the photos you take are stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

  • Photos: none collected. Photos you shoot are saved to your device (the app's album in your Photos library). They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): to understand how the app is used and to fix problems, the app sends usage events (for example: the app was opened, the camera became ready and how long that took, a photo was developed and with which camera, lens and film), an app-instance identifier, your device model and OS version, and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. This data is not linked to your identity, and the app does not collect any advertising identifier. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if the app crashes, it sends a crash report so we can fix the cause: the stack trace, the app's state at the time of the crash, your device model and OS version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier used to count how many devices a crash affects. Crash reports contain no photos and are not linked to your identity.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): if notifications are allowed, the app registers a notification token so we can tell you about new films. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Purchase: ROLLIE'S is a paid app. The purchase is handled by the App Store / Google Play, and the app never sees your card or account details. The app has no in-app purchases.
  • Store notices and app updates: the app periodically downloads a small text file (shop notices) from our static hosting, and checks our update server for app updates (the request carries the app version and platform). These requests contain no personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising, no tracking, and no photo/face data collection.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera — used only for the live viewfinder preview and taking photos.
  • Photo Library — used only to save your developed photos to your device and to show them in the app's album.
  • Notifications (optional) — only for news about new films.
  • Microphone — not used.

3. Retention and deletion

Because the app stores no photos on our servers, retention and deletion of your photos are entirely under your control on your device. Analytics data and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited period and are not linked to your identity; to ask about it, contact us below.

4. Third parties

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Analytics, crash reporting and notification delivery are provided by Google (Firebase) under its own privacy policy; payments are processed by Apple and Google under theirs.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
