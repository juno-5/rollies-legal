ROLLIE'S Privacy Policy

Effective: August 26, 2026 · Operated by MAYACREW Inc.

ROLLIE'S (the "app") is a film-camera style photo app. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and the photos you take are stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

  • Photos: none collected. Photos you shoot are saved to your device (the app's album in your Photos library). They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the app shows an optional rewarded ad when you choose to watch one for a free film. Google may process an advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. If you allow tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency), the ad identifier may be used for personalized ads; if you decline, you still get the same features with non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play. The app never sees your card or account details.
  • Store notices: the app periodically downloads a small text file (shop notices/banners) from our static hosting. This request contains no personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics SDK, and no photo/face data collection.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera — used only for the live viewfinder preview and taking photos.
  • Photo Library (add) — used only to save your developed photos to your device.
  • Tracking (optional, iOS) — only to show ads that match your taste. Declining changes nothing about the app's features. Your photos are never used for ads.
  • Microphone — not used.

3. Retention and deletion

Because the app stores no photos on our servers, retention and deletion of your photos are entirely under your control on your device.

4. Third parties

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob under its own privacy policy; payments are processed by Apple and Google under theirs.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page.
